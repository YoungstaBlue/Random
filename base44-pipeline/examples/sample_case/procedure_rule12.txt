PROCEDURAL RULE — Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 12(b)(6)

A party may move to dismiss a complaint for failure to state a claim upon which
relief can be granted. In ruling on a Rule 12(b)(6) motion, the court accepts all
well-pleaded factual allegations as true and draws reasonable inferences in favor
of the plaintiff. The complaint must contain sufficient factual matter to state a
claim that is plausible on its face.

The moving party bears the burden of showing that no claim has been stated. A
certificate of service must accompany the motion, and the deadline for a response
is governed by the local rules and any scheduling order entered by the court.
