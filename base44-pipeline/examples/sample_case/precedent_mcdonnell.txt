PRECEDENT SUMMARY — McDonnell Douglas Corp. v. Green, 411 U.S. 792 (1973)

The Supreme Court established a three-step burden-shifting framework for Title VII
employment discrimination claims lacking direct evidence. First, the plaintiff
must establish a prima facie case of discrimination. Second, the burden shifts to
the employer to articulate a legitimate, nondiscriminatory reason for the adverse
employment action. Third, the plaintiff may show that the stated reason is a
pretext for unlawful discrimination.

This framework is frequently applied in retaliation and wrongful termination
disputes. Courts applying stare decisis treat McDonnell Douglas as controlling
authority for the order and allocation of proof in disparate-treatment cases.
