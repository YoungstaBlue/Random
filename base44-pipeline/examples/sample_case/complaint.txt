COMPLAINT FOR WRONGFUL TERMINATION

Plaintiff Maria Santos brings this action against Defendant Acme Logistics Inc.
for wrongful termination in violation of Title VII, 42 U.S.C. 2000e. Plaintiff
was employed as a dispatch supervisor from 2019 until her termination on March 3,
2025. Plaintiff alleges she was discharged in retaliation for reporting unsafe
working conditions to the Occupational Safety and Health Administration.

The evidence includes a written safety complaint dated January 12, 2025, three
witness statements from co-workers, and email correspondence with the regional
manager. Defendant's stated reason for termination was a reduction in force, but
no other supervisors were laid off during the relevant period.

Plaintiff relies on the precedent established in McDonnell Douglas Corp. v. Green,
411 U.S. 792, which sets the burden-shifting framework for discrimination claims.
Under Fed. R. Civ. P. 8, Plaintiff must plead a short and plain statement of the
claim. A hearing on the motion to dismiss is scheduled and a response is due
within twenty-one days. The doctrine of stare decisis governs application of the
controlling precedent.
